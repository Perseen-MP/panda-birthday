🐼 Happy Birthday, My Lazy Panda! 💖
Forever & Always

Dearest Panda, Who would have thought that a random text to gather intel on a friend's crush would bring me the most precious, irreplaceable person in my world?

From sending that first nervous voice note saying my name, to that magical phone call that carried us through the morning right into the afternoon—every single memory with you is carved into my heart. You made late night conversations feel like minutes, and you gave warmth to ordinary days.

I call you "Panda" not just because you sleep 14 hours a day, passionately avoid baths, or behave like the world's most certified lazy fellow... but because you bring that same pure, chubby, heartwarming comfort to my life. You are truly an unexpected gem.

On this special birthday, I wish you endless peaceful naps, zero stress, delicious treats, cozy snuggles, and every ounce of happiness this world has to offer. Thank you for being yourself.

With all my love & endless pampering,
Your Favorite Human ❤️
